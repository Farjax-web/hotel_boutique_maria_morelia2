## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern colonial elegance rooted in the architectural heritage of Morelia's UNESCO World Heritage historic center. The aesthetic balances historic warmth with contemporary boutique luxury, reflecting the hand-hewn textures of *cantera rosa* (pink quarry stone), sun-warmed clay tile, and polished champagne brass.

The target audience comprises discerning domestic and international travelers, couples seeking romantic escapes, and architectural culture enthusiasts who prioritize authentic character, personalized hospitality, and understated luxury over sterile global chains.

The emotional goal is to evoke quiet sanctuary, warmth, and immediate trust. Key conversion patterns—real-time availability, clear suite tiers, curated experience add-ons, and secure direct-booking guarantees—are treated with editorial sophistication rather than aggressive commercialism. Visual devices marry subtle editorial framing, generous spatial breathing room, and soft textural warmth.

## Colors

The color palette directly references the historic palette of Morelia and traditional Michoacán materials:

- **Primary (`#A2574A` — Rosa Cantera / Terracotta):** Anchors primary call-to-action buttons, key price displays, active suite selections, and critical booking status cues. Provides rich architectural warmth without visual fatigue.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373` — Champagne Gold / Cantera Dorada):** Expresses understated luxury. Used for high-trust badges, rating stars, fine rule dividers, subtle focus highlights, and VIP amenity tags.
- **Tertiary (`#5A3825` — Roasted Cacao / Hand-Carved Madera):** Provides foundational typographic contrast for headlines and structural micro-accents. Imparts weight and permanence.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` — Warm Stucco / Cal Cálida):** Forms the foundational surface background, eliminating harsh digital white in favor of natural midday courtyard sunlight.
- **Surface Elevation Steps:** Container surfaces utilize `#FFFDF9` for elevated cards and `#F0E9DF` for secondary input fills and inactive structural dividers. Deep body text sits on `#2B231F` for accessible contrast against warm cream grounds.
- **Semantic Accents:** Urgency tags (e.g., "Only 1 suite left") use a rich saffron rust (`#B84A28`), while confirmation states utilize a heritage laurel agave green (`#4B634B`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical romance with crisp, high-clarity transactional UI readability.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Reserved for hero narrative statements, suite category names, pull quotes, and major section breaks. Captures the architectural grandeur of 18th-century palacios while retaining contemporary rhythm.
- **Transactional Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Deployed for all operational interactions—booking date selectors, room capacities, rate tables, filter tabs, cancellation terms, and form fields. It eliminates legibility friction during the critical checkout journey.
- **Label Caps:** Formatted with `text-transform: uppercase` and deliberate positive letter spacing (`0.12em`) to cleanly organize metadata (e.g., room sizes, occupancy limits, architectural details) without competing with serif titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large, atmospheric photography alongside high-conversion booking modules.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays span a max-width container of `1280px` with 12 fluid columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `3rem` (`48px`) outer margins. Breakpoint reflow occurs at `1024px` (tablet: 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters) and `640px` (mobile: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer margins).
- **Persistent Booking Engine:** On desktop, the date/guest selection bar sits horizontally suspended across the lower hero frame. On mobile, it condenses into a persistent bottom sheet trigger allowing immediate room checks from any point of scroll.
- **Rhythmic White Space:** Sections breathe using alternating large and extra-large spacing intervals (`space-xl` and above) to evoke the airy calm of an interior open-air colonial courtyard (*patio central*). Component interiors maintain compact, precise spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure conversion efficiency and tight data scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system avoids synthetic, cold shadows. Instead, it relies on warm ambient light diffusion and layered organic materials:

- **Ambient Shading:** Elevated surfaces cast extra-diffused, long-throw shadows tinted with warm earthen hues rather than neutral grays: `box-shadow: 0 14px 34px -6px rgba(78, 48, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(78, 48, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational background is Warm Stucco (`#F9F6F0`). Interactive cards and reservation panels rise onto White Lime (`#FFFDF9`), framed by soft, low-contrast sandstone borders (`rgba(162, 87, 74, 0.12)`).
- **Subtle Glazing:** Sticky navigation headers and floating mobile booking bars implement low-opacity frosted warmth (`background: rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) bound by a hairline gold accent border (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.3)`).
- **Interactive Depth Shifts:** Suite listing cards and CTAs elevate subtly on hover (`translateY(-2px)`) with an expanded warm shadow perimeter, signaling tactile response.

## Shapes

The design system specifies a Soft (`1`) roundedness profile. This aligns with the soft-hewn masonry of colonial stone architecture, avoiding both sterile sharp corners and overly playful pill geometry.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Standard elements use `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii, providing subtle physical softness while maintaining architectural structure.
- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** Room cards, photo frames, and review modules use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii.
- **Hero Windows & Gallery Features (`rounded-xl`):** Key architectural callouts and primary photography cards leverage `0.75rem` (`12px`) radii, occasionally referencing subtle Spanish colonial arch curvatures in curated media masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Reservation):** Solid Rosa Cantera background (`#A2574A`), crisp white Plus Jakarta Sans text, `font-weight: 600`, `letter-spacing: 0.02em`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover transition to `#8C473B` with micro-elevation.
- **Secondary Action (View Amenities / Virtual Tour):** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in Roasted Cacao (`#5A3825`), matching text color. Hover fills to `#5A3825` with neutral cream text.
- **Text Action (Discover Experience):** Champagne Gold (`#D4A373`) inline link with a continuous subtle underline sitting 4px below text baseline; underline expands to 2px on focus/hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Urgency Tags ("Only 1 Suite Available"):** Background tinted terracotta tint (`rgba(184, 74, 40, 0.1)`), border `1px solid rgba(184, 74, 40, 0.3)`, text `#B84A28`, styled in `label-caps`. Accompanied by a pulsing micro-dot.
- **High Trust Badges ("Best Rate Guaranteed", "Historic Center"):** Background in soft sand (`#F0E9DF`), border in soft Champagne Gold (`#D4A373`), text in `#5A3825`. Accompanied by refined line-art icons.
- **Amenity Filters:** Inactive chips have `#FFFDF9` fill with subtle `#E6DFD5` borders. Active chips swap to `#A2574A` fill with white text.

### Suite Cards
- Surface: `#FFFDF9`. Border: `1px solid rgba(162, 87, 74, 0.12)`.
- Layout: Asymmetrical split on desktop (60% image gallery, 40% details and conversion column).
- Details Hierarchy: Suite name in `headline-sm`, key architectural highlights (e.g., "Original 18th-century wood beams", "Cantera fireplace") listed with custom bronze bullet marks, dynamic price display in `price-display`, and immediate cancellation policy badge.

### Form Inputs & Date Picker
- Surfaces utilize clean off-white fill (`#FFFDF9`) framed by `1px solid #D8CFC4`.
- Labels float top-left in `label-caps` using Roasted Cacao tone (`#5A3825`).
- Active and focus states swap border to `#A2574A` with a soft outer ring in `rgba(162, 87, 74, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box/Circle frame: `1.5px solid #A2574A`. Checked state fills with `#A2574A` and displays a crisp white check or center pip. Roundedness matches base token scales.

### Bespoke Hospitality Components
- **Courtyard Concierge Strip:** A dedicated trust banner confirming direct booking perks (e.g., "Complimentary mezcal tasting on arrival", "Priority table at Restaurante María").
- **Historic Architecture Indicator:** An informational micro-badge detailing the historic restoration grade and date of each wing of the mansion.
